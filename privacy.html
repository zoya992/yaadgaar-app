🕯️ Yaadgaar Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2024

At Yaadgaar, we take your privacy and the security of your digital legacy extremely seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your email address to create and identify your secure account.
  • Encrypted Content: Messages, photos, voice notes, and trusted contact details. All content is End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE) on your device. We never have access to your encryption key.
  • Usage Data: Your last login timestamp and chosen inactivity period for the Dead Man's Switch feature.
  • Local Device Data: Your encrypted PIN verifier and app preferences are stored locally on your device only.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to create your account, store and sync your encrypted memories, execute the Dead Man's Switch protocol, and send essential service communications.

3. Data Security & Encryption

We use industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption. Your encryption key is derived from your password using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations entirely on your device. Because we never receive your password or key, we cannot decrypt your data.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use Supabase to store encrypted data and manage authentication. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your encrypted data as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all data anytime via Settings. Deletion is irreversible.

6. Children's Privacy

Yaadgaar is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at: support@yaadgaar.app
